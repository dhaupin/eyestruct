- Process-per-look: never a resident daemon. Browserless verbs (init/manifest/ledger/gate/adopt/raster) must never require a browser.

- The bridge/carrier ruling (2026-10-28): adapters and connectors are STRICTLY bridges - they move calls/events/artifacts between surfaces and never define, author, or evolve schema. Anything schema-touching lands as a CARRIER pack in packs/public/ under the packstruct@1 grammar first. lib/index.js names its carrier via SCHEMA_CARRIER (packs/public/eye-adapter); a bridge that outruns its pack is refused by name in the campaign.

- The walk-exit rule (the promotion rule, 2026-10-28): a manual walk is not done until nothing it found is uncommissioned. Walks DISCOVER classes; packs REMEMBER them.
